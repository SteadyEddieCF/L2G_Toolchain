# Known Limitations — SSP v1.9.13

- Very narrow mobile widths continue to suppress secondary toolbar controls according to the existing v1.9.12 responsive rules.
- Browser storage capacity and recoverability remain browser-dependent; this release improves only the warning presentation.
- Locally asserted review records remain unauthenticated and are not digital signatures, assessment results, certification decisions, or client-release approvals.
- Final Word QA remains outside SSP and requires the separately authorized RG-4 SSP/Builder-Merger handshake release.
