# Known Limitations v1.9.9

- Local reviewer names, roles, identifiers, attestations, and stage completion records are unauthenticated.
- No directory integration, access enforcement, digital signature, trusted timestamp, email/calendar integration, remote notification, or background workflow is provided.
- Manual dispositions are human assertions; the runtime does not determine technical accuracy, evidence sufficiency, control effectiveness, assessment status, readiness, risk, compliance, certification, or client-release approval.
- No preliminary or final Word inspection, final assembly, merging, or client-delivery packaging is implemented.
- Referenced external artifact currency is locally asserted through its stored fingerprint/current flag; the browser does not retrieve or verify remote artifacts.
- UX-3 unified Needs Attention is not included.

- Native Windows Chromium `file://` execution is pending independent draft-PR CI because this container blocks all browser navigation by administrator policy.
