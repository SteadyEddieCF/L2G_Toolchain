# Local Staged-Review Orchestration v1.9.9

## Ordered stages

1. Automated SSP source-data preflight.
2. Local SME Technical Review.
3. Conditional SME corrective action.
4. Independent Local Quality Review.
5. Conditional Quality corrective action.
6. Local Project Director sign-off.

All names and roles are locally asserted and unauthenticated. Stage progression requires terminal dispositions for applicable required items, current source and referenced-artifact fingerprints, required rationale for permitted N/A/exception results, closed corrective actions, and a local attestation. Advisory items are not automatically blockers. The Project Director local identifier may equal the SME reviewer local identifier, but may not equal the Engagement Lead local identifier.

Historical profile v0.1 preflight runs remain readable and unchanged. Profile v0.2 requires a non-mutating preview followed by explicit local confirmation.
