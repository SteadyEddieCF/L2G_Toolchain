# Known Limitations — SSP v1.9.14

- At widths below the supported desktop range, existing responsive rules may hide secondary controls such as Print before collapsing the main workspace.
- Scrollbars are visually hidden in the affected modal workspaces; users can still scroll by keyboard, mouse wheel, touchpad, touch, and programmatic navigation when overflow exists.
- Browser storage capacity and recoverability remain browser-dependent.
- Locally asserted review records remain unauthenticated and are not digital signatures, assessment results, certification decisions, or client-release approvals.
- Final Word QA remains outside SSP and requires the separately authorized RG-4 SSP/Builder-Merger handshake release.
