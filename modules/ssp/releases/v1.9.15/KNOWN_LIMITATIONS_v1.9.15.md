# Known Limitations — SSP v1.9.15

- The adaptive threshold uses viewport height, not physical laptop detection.
- The expanded/collapsed choice persists only for the current browser session.
- Very narrow mobile layouts continue to wrap the filters according to the existing responsive rules.
- Locally asserted review records remain unauthenticated.
- Final Word QA remains outside SSP and requires the separately authorized RG-4 SSP/Builder-Merger handshake.
