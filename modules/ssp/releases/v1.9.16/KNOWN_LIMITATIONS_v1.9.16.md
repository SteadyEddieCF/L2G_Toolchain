# Known Limitations — SSP v1.9.16

- Adaptive defaults use viewport height, not physical device detection.
- The explicit expanded/collapsed choice persists only for the current browser session.
- Very narrow layouts continue to stack setup controls under existing responsive rules.
- Locally asserted review identities remain unauthenticated and are not digital signatures.
- SSP does not perform final Word QA or client-release approval; RG-4 remains separately authorized SSP/Builder-Merger handshake work.
