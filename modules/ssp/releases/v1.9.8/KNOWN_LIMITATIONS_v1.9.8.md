# Known limitations — v1.9.8

- RG-1 checks source completeness and internal consistency only.
- Results are not an assessment, review approval, evidence-sufficiency determination, risk/readiness/compliance score, certification, or final-delivery decision.
- No DOCX is inspected; no Word-specific claim is made.
- Local names, roles, approvals, initials, and signatures are not authenticated.
- Built-in profile definitions are fixed; custom profiles are not supported.
- RG-2 staged review, corrective-action orchestration, UX-3 queue integration, sidecars, and final document QA are not included.
