# Known Limitations — SSP v1.9.7

- Portfolio mode remains an Advanced, browser-local workspace; it is not a server-backed collaboration system.
- Workspace state is local to the current browser/device and may be cleared by browser policy or storage cleanup.
- Locally asserted roles, reviews, approvals, and baselines are not authenticated identities or digital signatures.
- The Delivery view routes to existing SSP exports; Builder/Merger remains responsible for final assembly and Word QA.
- UX-3 unified Needs Attention, RG-1 review gates, staged review, Word inspection, sidecars, and guided bulk workflows are not included.
- Automated accessibility checks do not replace representative assistive-technology testing.
